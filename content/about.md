I'm Jorge Laborda, a machine learning engineer with a PhD in Artificial Intelligence. I focus on taking models from notebook to production: reproducible pipelines, infrastructure as code, experiment tracking and monitoring, applied to fintech problems like credit risk and financial document analysis.

Right now I'm building two portfolio projects end to end. **SmartWealthAI** is a credit and investing platform: an explainable credit default model served with FastAPI, tracked with MLflow, monitored for drift with PSI and deployed with Terraform on AWS ECS Fargate. **fintech-sec-rag-agent** is an AWS-native RAG pipeline that turns SEC 10-K filings into qualitative signals with Bedrock, OpenSearch and Terraform. I'm also preparing the **AWS Certified Machine Learning Engineer – Associate (MLA-C02)**.

Before this, I spent six years as a predoctoral AI researcher at the University of Castilla-La Mancha, where I earned my PhD (cum laude) on parallel and distributed approaches for learning Bayesian networks in high-dimensional problems. My algorithms ran up to 8× faster than strong baselines and led to several Q1 journal articles, and I built reproducible pipelines on a SLURM cluster with GitHub Actions CI/CD. I then worked briefly as a Data Scientist at Insight Solutions, on feature engineering and failure diagnosis for a client–agent scoring model.

**Stack:** Python, scikit-learn, XGBoost, TensorFlow, PySpark, MLflow, FastAPI, Docker, Terraform, AWS (Bedrock, ECS Fargate, S3, Step Functions), GitHub Actions, SQL.
